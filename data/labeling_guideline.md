# Labeling guideline (v2, "gamer" labels)

Labels are the README's three classes. Each message is judged on its own, as an
experienced LoL/Dota player would read it. Non-English messages are judged
the same way when the meaning is clear; otherwise 0.

## 0: neutral / positive
- Normal game talk, strategy, pings, questions, greetings, compliments, "gg", "gg wp", "nice", "thanks".
- Apologies and self-criticism: "my bad", "i suck", "sorry guys".
- **Swearing or venting at the game, an item, a mechanic or oneself, not at a person**: "oh shit nice play", "i can't fucking see minion hp", "this game is a mess", "fuck, missed".
- Banter that is clearly friendly, "ez" meaning easy to do something ("i hit ez with q"), "troll"/"feed" as a self-announced joke ("go go i go feed").
- Not understood, gibberish, or too little context to call it an attack.

## 1: negative attitude (toxic toward players, not identity-based)
- Insulting a person's skill or intelligence: noob, trash, useless, idiot, stupid, dumb, "u suck", "hardstuck bronze".
- Blaming or complaining about a teammate/enemy: "jungle diff", "u feed", "why u steal kills", "cant handle top dont pick top", "stop playing nasus", "shaco never engages".
- Taunting: "gg ez", "ez", "free elo", "rofl apes", mocking age ("9 years old kid").
- Reports and threats to report: "report mid", "please report lux".
- Aggressive profanity aimed at a person: "fuck you", "stfu", "shut up", "wtf was that injoker".
- Rudeness or dismissiveness: "nobody CARES", "just stop speak mundo".
- Sexual or violent insults that are not identity slurs, "cant wait to fuck ur mom", "go die".

## 2: derogatory (identity-based or severe)
- Slurs and identity insults: race, nationality/ethnicity used as an insult, sexuality, gender, disability.
  Examples: nigga/nigger, faggot, "u gay" as an insult, retard/tard, cunt, bitch/whore/slut used as an insult, "go back to your country", "fuck your russian", non-English equivalents (perra, kurvo).
- Telling someone to kill themselves ("kys", "go kill yourself"), wishing illness or death on them ("get cancer"), rape threats or sexual harassment of a person.

## Tie-breakers
1. Highest applicable class wins (2 > 1 > 0).
2. Target matters: profanity with no human target is 0; with a target it is 1; with an identity slur it is 2.
3. "bitch" as a plain exclamation with no target ("BITCH I LOOK LIKE GOKU") is 1 at most; aimed at someone ("carry more bitch") is 2.
4. When truly torn between 0 and 1, choose 0 only if a friendly or self-directed reading is natural; otherwise 1.

## Conventions added after the 300-message pilot
5. Criticising a hero/champion, item or the game itself ("broot is a shit hero", "critplank is useless") is 0. Insulting the person playing it is 1.
6. A slur word anywhere in the message is 2, even when quoting or complaining about someone else's use of it.
7. A frustrated demand with profanity ("SOME FUKING HELP?!") is 1.
8. Mild sarcasm or nagging with no insult or blame word ("is tryharding too much", "but u cant :)") is 0.
9. Bare questions about a play ("why you go solo", "where wardet?") are 0 unless they contain a blame or insult word.
