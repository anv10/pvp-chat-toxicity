# Format (shared by all lexicon files):
#   - one term or phrase per line, lowercase; '#' starts a comment

#   - a trailing '*' means prefix match: "faggot*" also matches "faggots"

#     handles leetspeak (f4g, n1gg3r), masking (f*g, n***a), stretched letters
#     (faaag), separators (f.a.g) and spaced letters (f a g)
